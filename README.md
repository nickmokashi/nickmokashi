# Hi, I'm Greygray.

I create books, software and strange digital worlds, and spend a suspicious amount of time asking what else technology could become.

## Public showcases

Most working source stays private while these public repositories show what each project is, what currently works, and where it is going.

### The website

#### [The Grey Strand](https://github.com/nickmokashi/the-grey-strand-showcase)

A website built as a house, at [thegreystrand.com](https://thegreystrand.com). Every room holds a different kind of work: articles, a podcast, books, a tarot deck, paintings, sound and games. Not every door is on the map.

### Books

#### [The Image of God and The Counterimage](https://github.com/nickmokashi/the-image-of-god-showcase)

Two research books built as negatives of each other. One tries to reconstruct the Source from the traces it left behind. The other walks the same ladder toward what opposes it.

#### [The Gospel of the Grey](https://github.com/nickmokashi/the-gospel-of-the-grey-showcase)

A gospel-shaped book in progress: seven levels of hell, the Intermission, seven levels of heaven, and the Final Return. Scripture found in a break room.

#### [Gradient Mining](https://github.com/nickmokashi/gradient-mining-showcase)

A research book about getting useful work out of the difference between things, and a Windows map of the research behind it.

### Software

#### [Synsemble](https://github.com/nickmokashi/synsemble-showcase)

A Windows desktop app that runs a small AI organization you own, staffs each job with the fewest workers it needs, checks the result independently, and shows the real work as a living building.

#### [Takesmith](https://github.com/nickmokashi/takesmith-showcase)

A local-first teleprompter and podcast studio that follows the person speaking instead of forcing the person to follow the script.

#### [Worksong](https://github.com/nickmokashi/worksong-showcase)

A small Windows app that scores your computer work in real time with continuous instrumental music, calmer when the work is quiet and busier when it picks up.

#### [Eonweld](https://thegreystrand.com/eonweld/)

A god game about land, intelligent life, and consequences across centuries. Shape a world, then watch its people make history. Free to play in your browser, at [thegreystrand.com](https://thegreystrand.com/eonweld/).

#### [ENDOCOSM](https://github.com/nickmokashi/endocosm-showcase)

A world built from what a person keeps. Journal entries, memories, beliefs, dreams and questions become lights in a place you can walk through. The universe expands outward. This one expands inward.

#### [The Living Sphere](https://github.com/nickmokashi/the-living-sphere-showcase)

An AI system at work, shown as a sphere of a million metal pins. Ask it something out loud and watch it listen, look things up, use its tools and answer, every step rippling across the sphere as it happens.

#### [Polaritium](https://github.com/nickmokashi/polaritium-showcase)

A 3D universe of opposing forces you can touch. Connect light and dark, twist and tune what joins them, and watch rings, crystals and small living forms emerge on their own. A Windows game; the AI is optional and has a hard spending limit.

#### [Venture Engine](https://github.com/nickmokashi/venture-engine-showcase)

A laboratory for small businesses, not a promise. It finds ideas worth testing, builds evidence, and kills weak ones early, because killing weak ideas is what saves money. Every budget starts at $0.

## I build with AI

AI is openly part of my creative and development process.

I do not use it to avoid having ideas. I use it to pursue ideas that would otherwise be too large, too strange, or too technically complicated for one person to chase alone.

Human judgment stays in the driver's seat.

## What the work explores

My work explores consciousness, spirituality, recovery, identity, suffering, humor, technology, and the strange territory between certainty and mystery.
